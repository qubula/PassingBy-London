## Facts

- 775 rooms, 78 of them bathrooms: the Royal Household's published figures
  for the palace (royal.uk).
- The Royal Standard flies when the monarch is in residence; the Union Flag
  flies when they're not (the practice since 1997).
- "Union Jack" is how a cabbie would say it; the official name is the Union Flag.

## Delivery

- A cheerful, chatty London cabbie, talking over his shoulder to a passenger.
  Quick, with a small pause before the punchline and a laugh in the voice on
  the last line.
- If it's ElevenLabs: lower Stability (around 30–40%) and raise Style
  (around 40–50%) for more expression. With the v3 model, audio tags such as
  `[cheerful]` at the start and `[laughs]` before the last line help.
- Record two or three takes and pick the liveliest.
